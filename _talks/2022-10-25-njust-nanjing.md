Isogeometric analysis (IGA) is a well-known computational paradigm that integrates geometric modeling and numerical simulation using a consistent mathematical representation. In IGA, constructing high-quality, analysis-suitable parameterizations from nothing more than a boundary representation is a fundamental task. In this talk, we present two robust and efficient parameterization methods.

Keywords: **Isogeometric Analysis**, **Analysis-Suitable Parameterization**, **r-Adaptivity**, **Curvature-Based Parameterization**
